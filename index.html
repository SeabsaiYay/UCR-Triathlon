Welcome to the UCR Triathlon Club! We are a community of athletes dedicated to training and competing in triathlons. Join us for group workouts, training tips, and social events.

About Us

The UCR Triathlon Club is open to all students, faculty, and staff at the University of California, Riverside. We welcome athletes of all skill levels, from beginners to experienced triathletes. Our club provides a supportive environment for training, learning, and competing in triathlons.
